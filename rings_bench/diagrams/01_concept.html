Concept flow

The same pile of transfers, no answer key, every model family takes a swing. Code gives every piece an id, the model chooses and explains, code checks and gates, a person sees the forks, PRISM holds the record.

data (files)code (deterministic, ours)a model (through Nicrron)a personPRISM (sponsor observability)the UI pagean honest limita notedashed border = assumed (not confirmed with the engine thread)

Who looks at this, for what

A judge in the first minute, to get the shape of the whole thing before any number. Teammates use it to say the same story in the same order.

What each element is for

  • Steps 1 to 4 answer "what did the models actually get" (a pile with ids, no key) and "what did they give back" (a verdict with ids and a reason).
  • Step 5 answers "who says it is right": code, against IBM's labels, never the model.
  • Step 6 is the screen the judge will see next; the words match the UI panels.
  • Step 7 is the one line the audits said the earlier Rings page buried: the rule row and the enriched rate, said before anyone asks.
  • Step 8 is the sponsor requirement (Money Operations: Sessions, Alert, Root Cause then v2) in one sentence, with the credits limit so nobody expects PRISM to have analyzed everything.

Assumed

"N in ten" is a placeholder until the bench results exist. The seven families and the two prompt versions are from the engine thread's design at 11:02; if a family is dropped for budget, step 3 changes.
