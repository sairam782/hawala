User interaction chart

Three people as swimlanes: the judge's 3 minutes and four likely questions, the analyst the product is for, and the teammate operating the bench. Dashed arrows are where one person's action lands on another's screen.

Who looks at this, for what

Whoever presents, to rehearse the 3 minutes and the four likely questions; the UX thread, to check every click named here has a screen; the engine thread, to check the rerun path exists for the last question.

What each lane is for

  • The judge: the six beats are the demo in order, matching the audits' advice to show the strong run, the failed run and the rule row rather than every arm. The four questions are the ones the brief expects; each dashed arrow points at the beat that answers it.
  • The analyst: the product's reason to exist. The audits said the earlier Rings page lacked "a concrete analyst action after a flag"; this lane is that action: search an account, a ranked list, follow the money hop by hop, see which models agree, decide, export the case. The data carries account ids and bank codes only, so "search a person" is not possible on this set.
  • The teammate: add a model, rerun, score, refresh, send, read. The "run it on something we choose" question lands on the rerun step, so that step has to be minutes, not hours (60 requests a minute, two workers).

Assumed (dashed boxes)

  • The teammate's file names (models.toml, run_bench.py, score_bench.py).
  • That the analyst's decision is written back beside the PRISM record. The bench design keeps the model's run; whether the person's decision joins it is not designed yet.
  • The analyst lane's search, follow-the-money, who-else-flagged-it and case-pack boxes are the request of 11:45 drawn as screens; the UI thread owns whether and how they are built.
  • That a judge-chosen rerun is possible in the room: it needs the runner to accept a subset of groups and one model, and the Nicrron budget to have room.

%% User interaction chart, Rings bench. Three people as subgraphs (swimlanes), time left to right.
flowchart LR
  subgraph JUDGE["The judge: 3 minutes, then questions"]
    direction LR
    J1["0:00 the network\nslice replays, no answer key"]:::ui --> J2["0:30 one model's rings light up\ngreen agree, red false, amber missed"]:::ui
    J2 --> J3["1:00 the leaderboard\nv1 | v2 per family, rule row, cost, latency"]:::ui
    J3 --> J4["1:30 failures by shape\nclick a cell for the runs"]:::ui
    J4 --> J5["2:00 the PRISM proofs\none v2 run's 7 steps, the fired alert, cluster then v2 green"]:::prism
    J5 --> J6["2:30 one honest limit\nACH tell, enriched rate, wrong N in ten"]:::limit
    Q1["Q: show me the PRISM trace"]:::person -.-> J5
    Q2["Q: how do you know it is the right row?\nid in slice = id in answer = id in the run's steps"]:::person -.-> J2
    Q3["Q: does it hold on new data?\nno claim; one slice, one run per cell"]:::person -.-> J6
    Q4["Q: run it on something we choose"]:::person
  end
  subgraph ANALYST["The analyst: a bank's financial-crime desk"]
    direction LR
    A0["search an account or bank\n(account ids and bank codes; no names in the data)"]:::ui --> A2["a ranked, checkable list\nring | ordinary, pattern, confidence, why"]:::ui
    A2 --> A3["follow the money\nin and out on a timeline, hop to the next account, breadcrumb"]:::ui
    A3 --> A3b["who else flagged it\nevery model's verdict and reason, cited ids highlighted"]:::ui
    A3b --> A4["decide: escalate or dismiss, with a note\na person decides; case pack exports"]:::person
    A4 --> A5["the record in PRISM (decision beside it: assumed)"]:::prism
  end
  subgraph TEAMMATE["The teammate: operating the bench"]
    direction LR
    T1["add a model\nmodels.toml (assumed)"]:::code --> T2["rerun\nrun_bench.py, 60/min, 2 workers"]:::code
    T2 --> T3["score\nscore_bench.py -> bench_results.json"]:::code
    T3 --> T4["refresh the UI\nnew row, new rings"]:::ui
    T4 --> T5["send to PRISM\ntraces, v2 trajectories, alert rules, analyze, Analyze now"]:::prism
    T5 --> T6["read the clusters\nRoot Cause, filter agent_name, one sentence why"]:::person
  end
  Q4 -.->|chosen groups| T2
  T4 -.->|new row| J3
  J2 -.->|same list, one model| A2
