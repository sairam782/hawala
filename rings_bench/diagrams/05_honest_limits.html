Honest limits map

Where the data lies to us, pinned to the pipeline step it bites: the ACH tell, partial labels inside bursts, the enriched rate, our pattern names, one run per cell, and 'rings found' read as cases. Each card ends with the sentence to say.

Who looks at this, for what

The presenter before Q&A, so each limit is said before it is asked; the judge who asks "does it hold on new data"; the UX thread, because the honest panel on the UI page should carry these six in the same words.

Why these six

  • The first five are the ones the brief named. The sixth (rings are not cases) is from the audits of the earlier Rings page: both reviewers read "rings found" as cases, and one noted that a person has to translate "10.5% precision" into "wrong nine in ten" themselves.
  • Two facts came from the data audit relayed at 11:40 and are folded into cards 1 and 2: ACH is 87% of laundering in the whole file (100% in our slice is a cut artifact), and the file's tail after day 10 is 59% labeled, touching four of our 20 ring units.
  • Each card ends with the sentence to say, so the limit is a line, not a confession.

Not drawn

Limits of the models themselves (a family that returns no JSON, rate limits mid-run) belong on the leaderboard as failed cells, not here. PRISM's own soft spot (its evaluator judge said right on wrong answers in practice) sits with the evaluator, now an option in the technical page's credits note.
