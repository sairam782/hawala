# Review 1: timedepth3d ("Rings in time")

Reviewer: Claude (first-loop review, 2026-10-17 about 13:20). Review only; no variant file was edited.

## Verdict

A real contender, and the engineering is sound. Every headline number matches an independent recount from the data files, for the default model and for `?model=openai/gpt-6-sol&v=v4`. The local three.js copy loads when the CDN fails. Each render is 8 draw calls, and the page does not redraw while idle. The tilt, scrub, catch tracing and roster are all built, with no particles, glow or swoops. It is not ready to show yet, for two reasons. First, the card and hover "shape name" is our structural reading (`ring_meta`), not IBM's `ibm_shape`; for group 8 the card says "mesh" while the model's own why on the same card says "gather-scatter". Second, the catch moment lands off-screen: at 1280x900 the roster starts at the bottom edge, so most copies fly below the fold. **The one thing that makes it a contender: the catch has to land where the judges can see it.** Put the roster in view, for example as a strip or side column inside the first screen. Do it together with the two-line fix that takes the shape name from IBM's `ibm_shape`, and the variant carries its whole argument in one screen.

## Findings (most severe first)

### High

1. **The shape name on cards and hover is not IBM's shape.** `app3d.js:62` (`ringShape` reads `SLICE.ring_meta[r].shape`), used at `:347` (card heading) and `:493` (hover title). I compared it with `ibm_shape` on each ring's own unit in `bench_results.json`. Five of 20 rings show a different name: group 8 "mesh" (IBM: GATHER-SCATTER, 11 of 12), groups 9 and 10 "chain" (IBM: STACK, fragment), group 11 "other" (IBM: STACK, 2 of 3), group 18 "lone" (IBM: BIPARTITE, 1 row). The 7 other one-row groups say "lone" where IBM says "no named shape". The worst case on screen: the gpt-6-sol v4 card for group 8 reads "mesh" above a why that starts "This resembles gather-scatter". The README's claim of "IBM's group number and shape name" is false for these rings. **Should be:** take the name from the ring unit's run `ibm_shape` (every run on a unit carries the same value), sentence-cased ("Gather-scatter, 11 of 12"). If our reading is kept, label it as ours.
2. **The catch flight lands below the fold.** `style.css:35` sets `.stage` height to `calc(100vh - 250px)`, so at 1280x900 the roster heading sits at y of about 800 and only the top half of the first card row is visible. With kimi v1's 19 cards that is 5 rows at 1280 and 4 rows at 1440, and cards are inserted in catch order, often in rows 2 to 5. The flyer (`app3d.js:357-368`) animates toward a thumbnail rectangle that is off-screen. My mid-Play capture shows the copy crossing the scrub bar on its way out of view. The brief's catch moment (copy travels to the roster) therefore mostly happens where nobody can see it. **Should be:** a roster that is visible without scrolling at 1280x900, for example a right-hand column under the result or a one-row strip of the most recent catches, or scroll the target card into view before the flight.
3. **A slow network blocks the whole page.** `index.html:10` (Google Fonts stylesheet) and `index.html:18` (cdnjs three.js) both sit in `<head>` and block rendering and parsing. I verified that a request which fails fast falls back correctly: with cdnjs and fonts aborted, `three.min.js` r160 loaded from the folder, there were no page errors, and first contentful paint came at 3.35 s under parallel load. A venue or captive-portal network that hangs instead of failing would leave the page blank until the request times out. **Should be:** load the bundled `three.min.js` directly (it is already in the folder, and this is a file:// demo), and make the font stylesheet non-blocking or local.

### Medium

4. **The default model is a weak opener.** `app3d.js:545` picks the model with the most rings caught, which is kimi-k3 v1: 19 of 20 rings, 104 of 113 transfers, 32 alarms. But 8 of those 19 rings are one-transfer groups, caught by flagging the one row. The first two roster cards are "lone, 2 accounts, 1 of 1 transfers traced", with a why about an 11-account fan-in. Its precision is 14% (628 wrong flagged transfers against 104 right), and it raised alarms on 32 of the 40 ordinary neighborhoods. Its one miss is group 0, the only cycle that lies entirely inside Sep 1 to 10 (see 7). **The default I would choose:** `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5-5` v4. It caught 12 of 20 rings and traced 100 of 113 laundering transfers (88%) with 2 alarms in 40 ordinary neighborhoods and 63% precision, all recomputed from the bench file. The page would end on "That is 88 percent of IBM's laundering transfers, traced", a strong number that a skeptical judge cannot turn against it. **Should be:** a fixed default (`model=anthropic/claude-sonnet-5-5&v=v4`), not "most rings found".
5. **The alarm count has no denominator, and precision is nowhere on the page.** `app3d.js:518`: "it raised 32 alarms on ordinary neighborhoods, 31 of them on accounts that sit inside a ring." It does not say "of 40". The second clause is true by its definition (`ring_accounts_flagged` is not empty), but it reads as a mitigation. Neither the copy nor the limit line carries a count of wrongly flagged transfers; the baseline page shows precision. **Should be:** "on 32 of 40 ordinary neighborhoods". Consider a short limit clause such as "k of its n flagged transfers were IBM's laundering".
6. **The timeline at 76 degrees smears the days.** The screen's horizontal axis is `0.24x + 0.97z` (`placeCamera`, `app3d.js:386-399`). The map half-width BX is about 62 units, so x moves a transfer up to plus or minus 15 units, against 10.7 units per day. A component at the map's edge can sit up to 1.4 days from its day label, because labels are projected at x = 0 (`app3d.js:410`). Each day frame becomes a band 2.7 days wide, so the timeline shows about 22 uneven vertical rules instead of 11 (visible in my 1280 and 1440 timeline captures). The teal "day the scrub has reached" frame becomes a box 2.7 days wide. Order inside one ring still reads, because a ring's accounts sit close together, but comparisons across the page do not. **Should be:** end the Timeline button at 88 to 90 degrees, or draw day ticks as single lines at the ring's own x, and draw the scrub as one line.
7. **On the default model, the caught cycle closes inside the squeezed slab.** `app3d.js:65,69`: Sep 11 to 16 gets `TAILZ = 7` units for 6 days, about 9 times tighter than the 11 units per day before it. Group 1 (cycle) runs from Sep 7 23:51 to Sep 11 16:44, so its return hop is compressed. Group 0 (cycle, Sep 1 to 5) is the full-length cycle, and kimi v1 missed it, so on the default it is drawn amber dotted. "A cycle comes back to its start" reads best on a model that catches group 0 (gpt-6-sol v4 does, 5 of 7). This is another reason to change the default.
8. **No time cue without the tilt.** At tilt 0 the camera is orthographic and colors do not change with time, so the map view shows no time at all apart from the scrub. The brief asks for depth cues that read without the tilt. **Should be:** a light-to-dark ink ramp by day on the lit segments, or on the hairlines.
9. **The agreement halo is absent** (stroke weight = families agreeing, on Alex's approved list). The only stand-in is the card line "Also caught by ...", and that line barely varies: most cards list 5 or 6 families, so it says little.

### Low

10. **Hover reveals a ring before its catch.** `app3d.js:477` skips a segment only when `CATCH.state[s.ring]` is set and is not 'shown'. A ring that is found but not yet reached has state `undefined`, so its hidden green pieces can still be hovered during Play, and the card says "Caught: ..." early. **Should be:** `CATCH.state[s.ring] !== 'shown'`.
11. **Lifelines appear before the catch.** Lifelines are built for found rings (`app3d.js:245-255`), but the catch state does not gate them, so during Play a ring's green lifelines show up before its shape draws.
12. **The legend does not match the strokes.** Lifelines are drawn in the group's color (green or amber at 0.45, `app3d.js:253`), but the key's "one account across time" sample is gray (`index.html` legend, `.k-life`). The amber "Sep 11 to 16" slab has no line in the key.
13. **Small gray text is under 4.5:1 contrast.** `--ink-3` #7A8296 on the paper ground is 3.58:1 (3.85:1 on card white, 3.29:1 on the controls bar). It is used for day labels (11 px), the view caption (11 px), the hint (12 px) and the card's group id (11 px). The amber tail label (5.04:1), teal (5.36:1) and green (4.9:1) pass. **Should be:** use `--ink-2` for those labels, or darken `--ink-3` to about #5F6880.
14. **Em dashes reach the screen through the model's text.** The variant's own text has none, but 23 of kimi v1's 60 whys contain one. They are shown as-is on cards and in hover (`firstSentence`, `app3d.js:19`). **Should be:** replace them with a comma when rendering.
15. **Wording.** The button says "Play the ten days" while the readout says "Sep 1 to 16". Card headings use raw lowercase ids ("lone", "fan-out"), not sentence case, and "lone" is not a reader's word. The view caption "map view: ..." starts lowercase.
16. **"Money moved" ranks currencies by face value.** `app3d.js:317`. For example, group 2 reads "1,553,050 Ruble + 5 more currencies", and yen and rubles outrank dollars on face value. The sums are correct (checked below), but "largest" means largest face value, not largest value. Either convert to one currency or say "in 6 currencies".
17. **The honest panel is thin.** One line (synthetic data, half-the-transfers rule). It leaves out the bench README's caveat most relevant to a "95 percent caught" headline: units were cut around IBM's rings, so they are easier than a blind scan. Keep it short and add that clause.

### Note

18. **Account search tie-in is absent** (optional on the approved list).

## Verified as correct

- **Numbers, default (kimi-k3 v1):** 19 of 20 rings, 104 of 113 transfers, 32 alarms, 31 inside a ring, 19 cards. **Numbers, gpt-6-sol v4:** 10, 68, 2, 2, 10 cards, ending "60 percent of IBM's laundering transfers, traced". My Python recount from `bench_results.json` (union of flags, half rule) gives the same values for all 26 model-versions, and all of them match `models[].rings_found` and `false_rings`.
- **Roster cards:** transfer counts and account counts are correct, and money moved equals the sum of the labeled rows' `paid` by currency for every ring. All 113 labeled ids are present in their own unit's rows. The why on each card comes from the ring's own unit.
- **The brief's required elements:** the tilt eases over 400 ms (`D(400)`, ease-out cubic). The day scrub extrudes forward through draw ranges. There are no auto-swoops, particles, glow or pulses (the hover card's soft shadow is the only shadow). One card per caught ring, in time order. The copy is catch-first, never opens with "wrong N times in ten", and ends on the stronger true percentage. The phrase "rings found" does not appear. `prefers-reduced-motion` and `?still=1` make every motion instant (checked by reading the code at lines 12-13 and 298). The variant's own text has no em dashes, and the spelling is American.
- **Console:** no errors in any run. One three.js warning: "build/three.min.js deprecated". Loading from the CDN worked; with the CDN aborted, the local copy loaded.
- **Performance** (headless Edge, SwiftShader software WebGL): 8 draw calls per render (merged hairlines, instanced tubes for lit lines). Idle: 0 draws in a second, with the loop still running at 147 callbacks per second. Playing: about 108 frames per second, redrawing every frame. First contentful paint: 2.3 s at 1440 with four headless browsers running at once; a single run loaded in 1.2 s. Payload: about 3.5 MB (bench_results.js 2.2 MB, three.min.js 656 KB, slice.js 392 KB, units.js 184 KB, app3d.js 40 KB).

## What I could not check

- The frame rate on a real GPU or projector, and any frame rate in a visible (non-headless) window.
- A network that hangs rather than fails: I simulated a fast failure only, by aborting the requests in Playwright. I deleted and edited nothing.
- The `msedge --headless --screenshot` CLI itself: I used Playwright driving the Edge channel at 1280x900 and 1440x900 instead. The probe and captures are in the session scratchpad (`probe/`), not in the project.
- Hover pick accuracy where lines cross. I hit only one hover target (group 8) in an automated sweep.
- How claude-sonnet-5-5 v4 looks on screen as a default (numbers recomputed only).
- Whether the "Also caught by" families match the baseline page's definition (any version counts here).
