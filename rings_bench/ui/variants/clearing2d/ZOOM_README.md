# Cluster zoom (zoom.js + zoom.css)

**What it does.** `window.openClusterZoom(unitId, origin?)` opens a close-up of one neighborhood over the graph stage (`#stage`, not the side rail). The neighborhood is laid out on its own (a small deterministic force layout over only its accounts) and fit to the stage with a margin.

- Every transfer is its own line with an arrow; parallel transfers between the same two accounts fan out as separate curves; self-transfers draw as loops.
- Colors: grey = ordinary; gold-green halo = IBM labeled laundering; green line = flagged by the selected model; teal-green line = flagged by the investigator (Claude Opus 5.5, from `INVESTIGATE.results`). Flagged by both = green line with a teal dashed center.
- Seed account (or, for rings with no seed, the hub account most labeled transfers touch) gets a teal ring and a label; accounts in an IBM ring are gold.
- Hover an account: id, bank, in/out counts and totals per currency inside this neighborhood, IBM ring membership. Hover a transfer: id, time, from, to, amount and currency, payment format, IBM label, model's call, investigator's call. Hover dims everything else. Cards are clamped inside the stage.
- Header: neighborhood name (seed), unit id, account and transfer counts, IBM shape if a ring, what was caught (model and investigator), first-pass verdict, confidence and reason, investigator verdict and reason. Reasons clamp to two lines; full text on hover (title).
- Time scrub at the bottom: shows transfers up to the chosen time.
- Close: Escape, the Close button, or clicking the dim margin. 250 ms in (fade plus grow from `origin` or stage center), 200 ms out; no animation under reduced motion.

**The hook.** `window.openClusterZoom('u06')`. Optional second argument `{x, y}` in client (viewport) pixels: where the neighborhood sits on the main canvas, so the zoom grows from there. `window.closeClusterZoom()` closes it. Selected model is read from `#model` (`id|version`), then `ST.sel`, then falls back to Claude Sonnet 5.5 v4.

**Checked (2026-10-17, headless Edge via Playwright, 1280x900, file://):** u06 (60 lines, 32 accounts, 12 labeled, 12 model-flagged), u19 (28 lines, 1 labeled, 3 investigator-flagged), u03 (17 lines, ordinary). Overlay exactly covers `#stage`; edge and account hover cards render and stay inside the stage; scrub halves to 30 of 60; Escape, backdrop and button all close; no console errors or warnings; no em dashes in zoom text (model reasons have them replaced with commas). Screenshots: `shots/zoom_u06_1280.jpg`, `shots/zoom_u19_1280.jpg`, `shots/zoom_u03_1280.jpg`, `shots/zoom_u06_hover_edge_1280.jpg`, `shots/zoom_u06_hover_node_1280.jpg`, `shots/zoom_u06_scrub_1280.jpg`.

**Known limits.** The click wiring from the main canvas is the other builder's (not tested here). The grow-in animation was not watched frame by frame; it was only checked that the end state renders. Reduced motion was not run in a test. Large neighborhoods (up to 46 accounts) label only the seed and ring accounts; others show their id on hover. The layout is fit by account positions, so a very long self-loop stack can poke slightly past the top margin. Model reasons over two lines are clipped in the header (full text in the tooltip).

## Second pass (14:13 ask): money movement and follow the money

- **Money movement, looping.** One canvas layer over the lines (`.zm-flow`, one requestAnimationFrame loop, no per-frame DOM changes). A dot travels payer to payee on every transfer line and loops, 1.6 s per trip, phase-offset per line. Dot radius is 2 to 5 px on a log scale of amount within the neighborhood. Gold-green = IBM labeled laundering, green = flagged (model or investigator), grey = ordinary. It respects the time scrub, stops on close, and does not run under reduced motion.
- **Follow the money.** "Follow the money" toggle in the header. Click one account, then a second. It searches directed paths of up to 4 hops through this neighborhood's transfers, each hop no earlier than the one before (same minute counts as in order). If none is in time order, it falls back to any directed path and says so. Paths are sorted by when the last hop lands; the earliest shows first, with "1 of N paths" and a Next path button (search stops at 400 paths). Everything else dims, hops get number badges, and a side list shows hop, time, id, amount and currency, format, from and to, IBM label and the model's call. Header: "Path: A to B, N hops, <sum of hop amounts per currency> moved". The dots then relay along the path only, one hop after another. Escape or the toggle clears the path; a second Escape closes the overlay. A third click starts a new pick.
- **Checked (headless Edge, 1280x900, file://):** u06 fan-out (2 hops, 1 of 3 paths), u19 lone (2 hops, 1 of 6), u47 cycle (4 hops, 2 paths, Next works). Badges and highlighted lines match the hop count. Escape clears and then closes. The dot canvas repaints between frames, and stays empty under reduced motion. No page console errors (the only warning came from the test's own getImageData readback). Shots: `shots/zoom_flow_1280.jpg`, `shots/zoom_path_1280.jpg` (u47, shown after Next so 2 of 2), `shots/zoom_path_u06_1280.jpg`, `shots/zoom_path_u19_1280.jpg`.
- **Limits.** The "moved" total adds every hop, so money that passes through several hops counts once per hop. The side list sits over the right edge of the graph and can cover accounts there. Paths are not offered from the main screen, only in the zoom. Frame rate was not measured.

## Third pass (15:28 ask): PRISM traces in the zoom

- index.html loads `../../../data/traces_demo.js` right after investigate.js, and every `?v=` is now `20261017-1535`.
- Under the verdict lines, one row per recorded run: "PRISM trace, first pass" (all 60 units, the Sonnet v3 run the page's v4 is built on) and "PRISM trace, investigator" (the 30 tray units, Opus v5). Steps show as small connected pills in order: a gear for tool calls, a violet diamond for the model call, a gold KB search pill, a check for the final answer, red with a cross for an error step. Hover or focus a pill to see its type, time, and in and out summaries. Each row ends with the agent name, "trace" plus the first 8 characters of the trace id, and a "recorded in PRISM" tag. Hovering the row shows the data note; hovering the agent and trace shows the full trace and trajectory ids. A unit with no entry shows no rows.
- Display labels are shortened to fit one line per row at 1280 (rows, chains, parse, answer, failed); hover shows the real step name.
- Checked: u06 (first pass only), u19 (both), u03 (both, first pass ends in a failed step, shown in red). Each row is one line, 17 px tall, and nothing is cut off. No console errors. Shot: `shots/zoom_trace_1280.jpg`.
